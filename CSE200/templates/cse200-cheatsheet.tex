\section{Technical writing}
Active voice; always ``we''. Short sentences.\par
One idea per paragraph; best sentence first.\par
Minimise notation. No $\forall \exists \in$ in prose.\par
Never start a sentence with a symbol.\par
Separate adjacent formulas with words (``where'').\par
Define symbols before use. Keep ``that''.\par
No contractions. Don't overuse commas.\par
Capitalise Theorem 1 / Figure 5 / Section 3;\par
\quad lowercase ``the following lemma''.\par
Spell small numbers as adjectives (five vertices);\par
\quad numerals as names (increased by 2; 80 embeddings).\par
Fact obvious (no proof) $\cdot$ Lemma proves others\par
Theorem main result $\cdot$ Corollary immediate.\par
Theorem statements must be self-contained.

\section{Research paper}
Read: 3 passes. P1 5 C's = Category, Context,\par
\quad Correctness, Contributions, Clarity.\par
P2 content, no details. P3 re-create the work.\par
Survey: high sensitivity / low specificity.\par
Salami science = one study, many papers.\par
IMRaD. Intro 5 Qs: what / why interesting /\par
\quad why hard / why unsolved / key components.\par
Methods + Results: past tense, no interpretation.\par
Prior work: present tense. Figures self-contained.\par
Abstract = mini paper; Methods often longest.\par
SPJ 7: don't wait write $\cdot$ identify key idea $\cdot$\par
\quad tell a story $\cdot$ nail contributions $\cdot$ related\par
\quad work later $\cdot$ readers first (examples) $\cdot$\par
\quad listen to readers.\par
Intro = 1 page: problem + contributions only.\par
No ``rest of this paper is''. Credit is not money.
